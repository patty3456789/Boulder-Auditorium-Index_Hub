System Notes & Reference

Quick Hits

  • Always check phantom power (+48V) status before plugging in condenser mics.
  • Verify gain staging before pushing faders up during soundcheck.
  • Mute unused channels to keep the noise floor low.

Vocabulary

  • Gain / Trim: Preamp control setting the initial input signal level.
  • Phantom Power (+48V): DC voltage supplied down the mic cable to power active electronics like condensers.
  • Bus / Subgroup: A routing pathway used to group multiple channels together (e.g., all vocal mics).

X32 Specifics

  • Use the Home and Config tabs to manage preamp gain, phantom power, and delay.
  • Assign DCA groups to physical faders for easy multi-channel volume control.
  • Save scene snapshots frequently before major show transitions.

BHS Specifics

  • Stage inputs route directly through the patch panel on stage left into the main stage box.
  • Main house speakers are permanently tuned for the Boulder Auditorium acoustics—avoid heavy graphic EQ boosts in the high-mid range.
